Your first 30 days

This is the onboarding plan for a new Overwatch Console client, from signed order form to the agents working the way your team wants. You start in approval mode, so nothing in your environment changes without a person approving it, and you decide when (and whether) any action runs on its own.

Before day 1

Have these ready. The Overwatch team checks them with you on a short kickoff call.

Day 1: connect and turn on

  1. Your first admin signs in and sets up two-step sign-in with an authenticator app (Getting started).
  2. The setup wizard connects your Sentinel workspace and runs the connection test. Setup does not deploy rules or change your workspace.
  3. On the Overview, the Turn on your SOC card turns on Dax (triage), Demitry (QA) and Marien (tuning proposals). Leave Renzo and Orion off for now.
  4. Invite your team from Settings > Team (Invite a teammate).
  5. Set up notifications so approvals reach the right people.

Week 1: watch the agents work

Everything stays in approval mode.

  • Each morning, open Approvals and decide on what is waiting (Approve or deny an action).
  • Open a few incidents Dax triaged and read the verdict, the queries it ran and Demitry's check (Review agent decisions).
  • When you disagree with a verdict, deny it or close the incident yourself with your reason. Tell us about anything that looks wrong; that is the most useful feedback you can give.
  • Ask Atlas questions about your environment (Ask Atlas).

Week 2: cut the noise and find the gaps

  • Review Marien's tuning proposals for your noisiest rules. Each one shows its replay against past incidents before you approve it (Tune a noisy rule).
  • Turn on Renzo and look at the coverage map in the Detection Library to see which ATT&CK techniques you have no detection for (Fix a coverage gap).
  • If you want containment (disable a user, isolate a device), set up response actions. They still wait for approval (Set up response actions).

Weeks 3 and 4: decide how much runs on its own

  • If your subscription includes Threat Hunting, turn on Orion for proactive hunts (Run a threat hunt).
  • Review your first weeks on Metrics & Health and in the Audit Trail.
  • On a review call with the Overwatch team, decide action by action whether anything should run in Full auto. Many teams keep everything in approval mode; that is fine. Changes are made in Settings > Agent Policies by an admin and are recorded in the Audit Trail (Set agent autonomy, Autonomy and approvals).

Onboarding is done when

  • Sentinel is connected and the connection test passes.
  • Dax and Demitry run on every new incident.
  • Your team has made at least one approval decision and one denial.
  • Notifications reach the people who approve.
  • You have decided, per action, approval mode or Full auto.
  • Your team knows how to reach support (Support).

If you leave

Ask us to delete your organization's data; we confirm in writing when it is done (How your data is handled). Deleting data in Overwatch never touches your Sentinel workspace. Remove the Overwatch apps from Microsoft Entra ID to end our access.
