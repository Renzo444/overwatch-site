# Help

Help shows these guides inside the console, for the version you are using, and tells you how to reach support.

## Where to find it

- Top bar: the **Help and support** button (the question mark). It offers **Guide to** the page you are on, **All guides**, **Email support** and **Keyboard shortcuts**.
- Command palette (Ctrl+K or Cmd+K): **Go to Help**.
- Sign-in page: "Trouble signing in?" with the support address.

## Who can use it

Everyone who can sign in: viewers, analysts and admins see the same guides.

## What you see

The page header reads "Help" with the line "Guides for every page of the console, written for the version you are using. Can't find an answer? Email support." and an **Email support** button.

**Guides.** On the left, **Search the guides** and the guides in four groups: **Start here**, **Concepts**, **Pages** (one guide for each page of the console) and **How-to guides**. Under them, **Support** gives the address to write to.

**The guide.** The guide you picked. Links to other guides open here. A guide about a page of the console has a **Go to** button that opens that page, and **All guides** goes back to the start. At the end, the guide says when it was last checked against the console, with a link to tell us if something doesn't match what you see.

**Search.** Type words and press Enter. The results list every guide that mentions all of them, guides with the words in their title first, each with the passage where they appear. **Clear** goes back to the guide you were reading.

## Support

Write to **AskOverwatch@overwatchsecurity.tech**. **Email support** opens a message with the page you were on and your organization filled in. Say what you did and what you expected to happen.

## Good to know

- The guides come with the console, so they change when the console is updated.
- Email support opens your own email program; nothing is sent from the console.
