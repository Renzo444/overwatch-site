Support

How to reach the Overwatch team, when we answer, and what happens outside support hours. Your order form names your plan; where a plan changes the answer, the table says so.

How to reach us

  • Email AskOverwatch@overwatchsecurity.tech.
  • Or select Help and support (the question mark in the top bar) and Email support, which starts an email for you (Help).
  • For something urgent, put URGENT in the subject.

Support hours

Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm US Eastern, except US public holidays. Autonomous plans also get extended hours, as set in the order form.

The agents work around the clock. Support hours are when people answer questions and fix problems.

Response times

The time to our first reply from a person, counted within support hours.

Availability

We aim for 99.5% monthly availability of the console, not counting announced maintenance or outages at Microsoft, the AI model providers or your own systems.

Outside support hours

Dax keeps triaging and Demitry keeps checking. Anything that needs a decision waits in Approvals and reaches the people you chose in notifications, so nothing consequential happens without a person unless your admin turned on Full auto for that action (Autonomy and approvals). Every action is in the Audit Trail.

If your daily AI budget runs out, the agents pause until the next UTC day and admins are told. Ask us if you need it raised.

Changes and maintenance

We tell your admins about changes to what the agents do, and announce planned maintenance in advance.

Security incidents

If we confirm a security incident affecting your data, we tell your security contact within 72 hours with what happened and what we are doing about it. How we store and protect data is in How your data is handled.
